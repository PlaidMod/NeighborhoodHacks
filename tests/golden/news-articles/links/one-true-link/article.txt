Lone Star College trustees will hold a public hearing on the proposed tax rate for tax year 2026 at their Oct. 1 meeting.

The board is also scheduled to vote on levying the maintenance and operations component of the ad valorem tax rate.

Residents can speak during the hearing by signing up before the meeting.

The college system also plans to replace the roof of the CyFair Center for the Arts building, a project that appears later on the same agenda.
